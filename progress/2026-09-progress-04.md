# Progress Update #4 (September 2026)

**Status:** Draft — financial reconciliation and required video pending

**Period covered:** 1 August–30 September 2026  
**Prepared:** 2 October 2026  
**Progress video:** Not yet recorded; required before final submission

## 1) Summary

During August and September, we continued operating NurseLink's home nursing service and developing Meridian, our care operations platform. We deployed web booking and coordination infrastructure, converted the Joo Chiat Social Club trial into a six-month paid pilot, and progressed a pilot with Nurse360. Our next focus is to validate booking fulfilment and turn pilot usage into recurring paid adoption.

## 2) Objectives recap

Our objectives remain to reduce friction in home-care intake and fulfilment, develop Meridian for care providers, strengthen verification and staff escalation, and convert suitable B2B discussions into structured pilots or recurring arrangements. B2C operations continue to inform the product through practical experience with matching, scheduling, payments and documentation.

## 3) B2C home nursing progress

Our cumulative internal figures reported as at **28 September 2026** were:

- More than **100 nurses**
- More than **80 platform families/users**, which should not be read as a count of paying families
- More than **150 completed care hours**
- More than **S$5,000 in gross booking value**

These are cumulative reported figures rather than August–September-only results. Gross booking value includes provider payouts and should not be read as company revenue.

The latest internal monthly report records **13 completed visits in September**, compared with **9 in August**. These monthly figures are provisional and remain subject to reconciliation with booking and financial records. They are presented separately from the dated cumulative snapshot above.

On 27 September, we deployed a web one-off booking flow connecting structured intake, staff clinical review, live Stripe checkout and publication of eligible bookings to the nurses' Telegram channel. Operational validation and measurement of completed bookings through the new flow remain priorities. Mobile rollout is being tracked separately.

We also progressed Coordinator, our assisted WhatsApp intake and quoting workflow, with escalation to staff. Clinical review and operational oversight remain important safeguards as we develop this workflow.

## 4) Meridian B2B platform development

Meridian development continued around intake, staff assignment, scheduling and routes, documentation and reporting, and resident records covering vitals and care notes. We are adapting these workflows to assisted living, private home care and community care. Feedback from service delivery and pilot users is helping us identify which coordination and documentation tasks need further work.

The next validation step is to measure actual use, documentation completeness and coordination effort, so that further development responds to observed operational needs.

Workflow discussions continue; pilot scope remains to be agreed.
