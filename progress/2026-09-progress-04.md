## 1) Summary

During August and September, NurseLink moved from product development and service coordination toward live booking infrastructure and paid Meridian pilots. We activated the one-off web booking workflow in production, progressed six-month paid pilots with Joo Chiat Social Club and Nurse360, and continued community-care validation. Community outreach included GIF, SAIA and TCA participation and September volunteering with the Singapore Women’s Association at the Society for the Aged Sick. These milestones support our next phase: reliable booking fulfilment, measurable pilot adoption and conversion into recurring paid usage.

## 2) Objectives recap

Our objectives remain to reduce friction in home-care intake and fulfilment, develop Meridian for care providers, strengthen verification and staff escalation, and convert suitable B2B discussions into structured pilots or recurring arrangements. B2C operations continue to inform the product through practical experience with matching, scheduling, payments and documentation.

## 3) B2C home nursing progress

By late September, our nurse network had grown to **more than 100 nurses**, compared with more than 80 in the July update. We had approximately **80 registered/onboarded families and caregivers**. The founder’s 1 October clarification put the number of families that had completed or paid for care at approximately **30**; registrations and families that completed or paid for care are tracked separately.

Our operational reconstruction indicates approximately **199 completed care hours through 30 September**, compared with more than 100 hours reported in July. These are approximate cumulative operational figures, rather than August–September-only results or audited accounts. The dated October addendum below records subsequent care activity.

On **27 September**, we activated the one-off web booking workflow in production, connecting structured intake, staff clinical review, live Stripe checkout and publication of eligible bookings to the nurses' Telegram channel. The production rollout also added match-before-payment sequencing. Next steps are to validate fulfilment, measure completed-booking conversion and progress the separate mobile rollout.

We also progressed Coordinator, our assisted WhatsApp intake and quoting workflow, with escalation to staff. Broader activation remains subject to security testing, real-family workflow validation and staff oversight.

## 4) Meridian B2B platform development

Meridian development continued around intake, staff assignment, scheduling and routes, documentation and reporting, and resident records covering vitals and care notes. We are adapting these workflows to assisted living, private home care and community care. Feedback from service delivery and pilot users is helping us identify which coordination and documentation tasks need further work.

The next validation step is to measure actual use, documentation completeness and coordination effort, so that further development responds to observed operational needs.

## 5) B2B discussions and deployments

### Joo Chiat Social Club / Camino House

The August trial progressed into a **six-month paid Meridian pilot**, as recorded in our September company materials. This advances the July objective of moving the assisted-living trial into paid use.

### Nurse360

Our September company materials record a **six-month paid Meridian pilot from 10 August**, extending validation to a home-care operator. The next objective is sustained use and conversion to a longer-term subscription.

### Queen's Life Guards Corp

Queen's Life Guards Corp (QLGC) has been using Meridian for community-care workflows through the SparkX+Change engagement. The initial live session took place on **26 July**, and use and validation continued during this reporting period. Our [16 September public update](https://www.instagram.com/nurselinksg/p/DdYArBlkVUW/) reported Meridian being used to support **more than 80 seniors**. This community-care reach is separate from the B2C platform families/users above.

### Hospital-at-home opportunity

We continued exploratory workflow discussions and prepared a September product roadmap for an anticipated RFQ, covering care coordination, implementation and validation. This remains a pre-award opportunity, with scope subject to the formal procurement requirements.
